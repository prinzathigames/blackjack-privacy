Privacy Policy for BlackJack - Just for Fun

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BlackJack - Just for Fun ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Who runs this app

BlackJack - Just for Fun is developed by Prinzathi Games. If you have questions about this policy, contact prinzathigames@gmail.com.

Information we do NOT collect

BlackJack - Just for Fun does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information directly.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following information locally on your device only, to remember your progress between sessions:

  • Game statistics (wins, losses, ties, streaks)
  • Vegas Mode chip balance and related bonus timers
  • Sound and haptics preferences
  • Which game mode you last played

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any server we control.

Advertising

BlackJack - Just for Fun displays advertisements (interstitial and rewarded video ads) using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To support personalized advertising, AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA) and other information as described in Google's own privacy policy.

On iOS, the App requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before any tracking-based advertising identifier is used. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For details on what Google collects and how it is used, see: Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's data disclosure.

Children's privacy

BlackJack - Just for Fun is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not involve real-money gambling of any kind — Vegas Mode uses a virtual, in-app chip balance only, with no way to purchase chips with real money or withdraw chips for real money or prizes.

Data retention and deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App from your device. This permanently removes all locally stored statistics, balances, and preferences.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at prinzathigames@gmail.com.
